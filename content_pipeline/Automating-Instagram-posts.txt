Instagram vs Facebook — two Graph calls

ENV (integrations/.env) — token MUST start with EAA:
  META_APP_ID=
  META_APP_SECRET=
  META_ACCESS_TOKEN=EAA…
  FACEBOOK_PAGE_ID=
  IG_BUSINESS_ID=
  MEDIA_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://your-bucket…/folder
  PUBLISH_DRY_RUN=true

1) INSTAGRAM Reels (our script: cli/publish/ig_publish_reels.py)
  POST /{IG_BUSINESS_ID}/media
    media_type=REELS & video_url=… & caption=… & access_token=…
  → poll GET /{creation_id}?fields=status_code until FINISHED
  POST /{IG_BUSINESS_ID}/media_publish
    creation_id=… & access_token=…

2) FACEBOOK Page Reels (lib/publish/facebook.py) — optional
  POST /{FACEBOOK_PAGE_ID}/video_reels
    file_url=… & description=… & access_token=…
