# sh03 — Focus versus tunnel vision

## YouTube

**Title:**
Focus becomes tunnel vision — here's the exit

**Description:**
When you start with focus it becomes tunnel vision — because you want to finish what you started.

What you started no longer looks like you thought. It's not contributing. It didn't work. People weren't responding — so we stopped.

Then you question yourself, feel depressed, and three weeks later you revive the same loop.

You have to keep track. Write the thought down. Capture where it came from. That gives a different perspective on your position in the world you're looking at.
