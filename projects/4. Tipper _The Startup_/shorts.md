## Short 3: The Brutal 80/20 Reality of the Service Industry

- **Theme:** Behind-the-scenes stress & margin fragility in hospitality
- **Target Duration:** ~65 seconds
- **Source Timestamps:** `36:20` – `37:05` + `38:40` – `39:20`

### Selected Spoken Segment
> "80% of the job is preparation, and you have to rely on multiple parts of the world to support it... you're clipping 20% of your margins simply because costs increase... and if that 20% where customers are satisfied isn't met, all of that preparation is burnt. You prepared food for today—if you don't sell it today, you can't sell it next year."

### Editing & Assembly Instructions
1. **Hook (0:00–0:05):** Start on `38:40`: *"80% prep, 20% customer satisfaction—and if you fail the 20%, everything is burnt."*
2. **Body (0:05–0:50):** Stitch `36:20` (managing multiple suppliers, razor-thin margins) with `39:03` (perishable product vs manufactured goods).
3. **Visuals:** Add fast-paced sound effects and bold captions highlighting cost percentages (**20% Margin**, **80% Prep**).

## Short 6: The Triangle of Systems: Government, Society, Economy

- **Theme:** Foundational Systems Thinking for Founders
- **Target Duration:** ~60 seconds
- **Source Timestamps:** `09:15` – `10:25`

### Selected Spoken Segment
> "You have to play within the triangle: Government, Society, and Economy. Where does your idea fit? Will people accept it culturally? How will you navigate legalities? ### Editing & Assembly Instructions
1. **Hook (0:00–0:06):** Start at `10:00`: *"Most systems extract value. Real entrepreneurs build systems that create it."*
2. **Body (0:06–0:48):** Use the diagram explanation from `09:15` to `09:55` showing the 3 pillars.
3. **Takeaway (0:48–1:00):** Emphasize closing sustainable loops rather than burning resources.
