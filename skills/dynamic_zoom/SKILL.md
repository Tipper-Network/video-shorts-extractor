# Skill: Dynamic Zoom

Polish on a **signed** short or chunk. The zoom does not find the cut, and it does not run on a timer.

## When

After `script_approved` and after the picture exists. Not during extraction.

## What a zoom is for

A zoom marks a turn the sentence already made: the contradiction, the admission, the land. One or two in a 50–75s short. A chunk can take a few more, still on turns, not on a clock.

## Parameters

- `zoom_factor`: 1.15. Enough to feel the lean. Not a punch-in that crops the whiteboard off a lecture.
- Hold about 2 seconds, then back to 1.0.
- Anchor on the speaker. On a board lecture, keep the board in frame. Do not center-crop a diagram out of the shot.

## Does not

- Shift every 3–5 seconds.
- Cut the audio.
- Choose in or out points.
- Stack with a sound effect on the same word. Pick one.
